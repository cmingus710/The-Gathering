# What's new for members (Phase 13)

## Settings

- **On a phone:** tap your initial in the top right, or **More → Settings**.
- **On a computer:** use the button with your name at the bottom left of the menu.

Settings has your profile, how you sign in, notification choices, this device's push notifications, appearance, directory privacy, and your roles.

- **Appearance:** Light, Dark, or System. System follows your phone or computer, including when it switches at sunset. The choice is saved on each device.
- **Roles and access** shows what you can do in the app. It's read-only: if something looks wrong, or you need access for a ministry, ask a church administrator.

## Notifications

Tap the bell to see your latest notifications without leaving the page.

- Opening the list doesn't mark anything as read. Tap a notification to open it (that marks it read), or use **Mark all as read**.
- Prayer, care, message, and youth notifications show a general label in the list, such as "Prayer update", so nothing private shows on screen. Open one to see it.

## Calling, texting, and directions

On a directory profile, the details someone shares come with buttons: **Call**, **Text**, **Email**, **Map**, **Directions**, and **Apple Maps**. Only details they chose to share ever appear.

## Photos and videos in discussions

When you start a discussion or reply, choose **Add photos or video**.

- You can add up to 6 files per post, including up to 2 videos.
- Photos can be JPEG, PNG, WebP, or iPhone HEIC, up to 20 MB each.
- Videos must be MP4 (H.264), up to 50 MB each. An iPhone `.mov` or HEVC video can't be played on every device; export it as MP4 (Most Compatible) first.
- You can describe each photo for people who use screen readers.
- You can cancel or retry an upload, or remove a file before posting.
- Location and camera details are removed from photos. Videos are kept as you upload them, and some phones store the recording location inside the video file. If that matters, turn off location for your camera before recording.
- Files are only visible to people who can see the discussion. In a group, that means group members only.

## Sunday lesson workbook

Open a lesson and choose **Open workbook** to follow along during the service.

- You can expand Scripture, answer the questions, and keep private notes.
- Answers save as you type. **Saved** means it's stored. If the connection drops, your text stays on the page and the app keeps trying.
- If you wrote in another tab or on another device, you'll be asked which version to keep. Nothing is overwritten without asking.
- **Your answers are private.** Teachers, administrators, guardians, and other members can't see them.
- Find past lessons under **Sunday lessons → My workbooks**. You can delete your answers there at any time.
- If a teacher rewords a question after you answered, your answer stays, along with the wording you answered.

## Books

- A book page has **Read on Kindle** and **Listen on Audible** (each only when the church has added that link). These open Amazon or Audible (or, on a phone with the app installed, your device may offer the Kindle or Audible app), where you sign in with your own account. You need your own access to the book; the church's copy doesn't give you access, and the book can't be read or played inside the app.
- Record where you are (Want to read, Reading, Finished, plus an optional note such as "Chapter 4").
- **Write privately** keeps notes in My Journal.

Your progress and notes are private.

## Campaigns

**Give** shows the church's current campaigns, with a progress meter. The total includes online, cash, and check gifts, less refunds. It never shows who gave or how much. Online giving is still in test mode during the pilot.
