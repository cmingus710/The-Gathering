# Bible reading and My Journal (for members)

Open **Bible Reading** from the home menu (the Bible tile), or **My Journal** from the menu under "You".

## Reading plans

- **Bible reading** (`/bible`) shows today's reading for each plan you've joined, the church's featured plan, other plans you can join, your bookmarks and highlights, and plans you've left.
- **Join a plan.** For a plan with numbered days, choose your start date and time zone; "today" follows that time zone. For the church's dated plan, dates follow the church's time zone; if it has already begun you choose to start from today or include the earlier readings.
- **Today's reading** opens the passages in your preferred version (see below), labelled with the version's name. Mark it **read** when you've read it (opening a reading never marks it). Select again to undo. Rest days never count as missed.
- **All days** lists the whole plan by month (or by 30 days), with what you've read.
- **Catch up** lists earlier readings you haven't marked, to read in any order.
- **Several plans** at once are fine; your **main plan** is the one on the home page.
- **Leave a plan** to stop it and its reminders; what you've read is kept and you can rejoin. **Delete my history** removes your progress for that plan (journal entries stay).
- Progress is private: nobody else sees it, and there are no comparisons with other members.

## Reading the Bible

**Open the Bible** (`/bible/read`) goes to your preferred version (or the church's, if you haven't chosen one). Choose a book and chapter, or use the previous/next links.

- **Reading settings**: text size, line spacing, and verse numbers; saved to your account.
- **Select verse numbers** to highlight (yellow, green, blue, pink), bookmark, or **Write privately** about them.
- The translation's name and required notices appear under the text. Some translations don't allow copying or printing; the reader respects that.
- If a translation's source is unavailable, the page says so. It never shows another translation or made-up text instead.

## Choosing your Bible version

When the church offers more than one version (for example the **World English Bible, WEB** and the **American Standard Version, ASV**), choose the one you prefer under **Bible reading → Bible version** and select **Save**.

- **Open the Bible** then opens your version. Switching version in the reader also saves it as your choice.
- **Plan readings** are shown in your version. If a passage is numbered differently in your version (Romans 16:25–27, for example), that passage is shown in the plan's version instead, and the page says so.
- On a reading's page, the **Version** links (WEB · ASV) switch just that page, without changing your choice.
- Every passage shows its version's code, and the version's name and notice appear underneath. Verses from different versions are never mixed in one passage.
- Choose **No preference** to go back to the church's version (and each plan's own version for plan readings).

Your choice is private, like your other reading settings.

## Reflection and discussion

A reading may have reflection questions. **Write privately** opens a new journal entry for that question, visible only to you. **Join discussion** (when there is one) opens the linked community discussion, where anything you post is seen by its members, like any post. Nothing you write privately is ever posted for you.

## My Journal

- Write entries on their own, or about a passage, a plan day (and one of its questions), a devotional, or a Sunday lesson (**Write privately** on those pages).
- **Autosave**: your entry saves to your account about a second after you stop typing, and when you leave a field or press Ctrl/⌘+S. The status line says **Unsaved changes**, **Saving…**, **Saved**, or **Not saved**. It only says Saved once the church's server has confirmed the exact text on your screen.
  - **Connection problems**: it retries automatically; if you're offline it waits and tells you. Your text stays on screen.
  - **Signed out**: it stops and keeps your text. Sign in again in another tab, then choose **Retry**. Don't close the page until it says Saved.
  - **Edited in two places**: if another tab or device saved a newer version, nothing is overwritten. Choose **Keep mine**, **Use the other version**, or **Keep both** (your text becomes a separate entry).
  - Leaving the page with unsaved text asks you first.
- **Formatting**: bold, italic, lists, and quotes, with a preview.
- **Search** your own entries (search words aren't put in the address bar).
- **Archive**, **restore**, **remove a link**, and **delete** entries. **Download** your journal (Markdown) or everything (JSON). **Delete my whole journal** asks you to type DELETE.
- If something an entry is about is later removed or you lose access to it, your note stays and says the content is no longer available.

## Who can read my journal?

Only you. Not church staff or administrators, group leaders, teachers, content editors, finance staff, or people in your household. There is no guardian access. Your journal is stored in your church account (so it's on every device you sign in on) and protected by access controls; it isn't end-to-end encrypted. See [journal-privacy.md](journal-privacy.md).

## Reminders

On a plan's page, under **Your settings for this plan**, switch on **daily reading reminder**, choose the time and time zone, and pause or stop whenever you like. Reminders:

- arrive at (or shortly after) your chosen time, at most once a day, and never more than 3 hours late;
- skip rest days, days with no reading, and days you've already read;
- say only "Your Bible reading for today is ready" in email and push (never the plan, passage, or anything from your journal);
- follow your notification settings (in the app, email, push) and quiet hours (**Notifications → Settings → Bible reading**).

Push to phones arrives with Phase 12; web push and email work now.
