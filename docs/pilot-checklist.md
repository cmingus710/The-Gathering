# The Gathering app: pilot tester checklist

Thank you for helping us try the app before the whole church uses it. It takes about 30–45 minutes, and you can spread it over a few days.

**Before you start**

- This is a trial. Please don't post anything private or confidential while testing; use `[TEST]` at the start of anything you create.
- **Giving is a test.** No real money is taken and real cards won't work. Keep giving the usual way.
- Notifications arrive in the app (the bell) and by email, not as phone pop-ups.
- Found something wrong or confusing? Open **More → Report a problem** (on a computer, it's in the left menu). Say what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. Only the church administrators see reports.

Tick each item, or note what went wrong.

## 1. Getting in

- [ ] Go to **www.gathering.church**, choose **Create an account**, and register.
- [ ] Open the verification email and tap the link.
- [ ] Sign in. You'll see a "waiting for approval" page until an administrator approves you.
- [ ] After approval (you'll get an email), sign in again and reach the home page.

## 2. Your profile and privacy

- [ ] **Profile → Edit profile details**: add or change something, save.
- [ ] **Profile → Directory privacy**: look at what others can see; change one setting.
- [ ] **Profile → Preview my directory profile**: does it show only what you chose?
- [ ] **Directory**: find another tester by name.
- [ ] If you live with another tester: **Profile → Household**, invite them, and accept.

## 3. Community and prayer

- [ ] **Community**: read the guidelines, start a `[TEST]` discussion, and reply to someone else's.
- [ ] Attachments in posts aren't available yet; check the composer says so clearly.
- [ ] **Prayer → Share a request**: share a `[TEST]` request with church members; add a comment or tap **I'll pray** on someone else's.
- [ ] Save a private draft and check another tester can't see it.

## 4. Events

- [ ] **Events**: open an event, register if it allows it, then cancel.
- [ ] Add an event to your calendar (the calendar download or subscribe link) and check the time is right.

## 5. Reading and learning

- [ ] **Bible reading**: open a plan and a day's reading.
- [ ] **Devotionals**, **Sunday lessons**, **Church reading**: open one of each; open a handout or attachment if there is one.
- [ ] **Memory verses**: open a verse and try a practice.

## 6. Music, podcasts, and photos

- [ ] **Music**: press play on a playlist; tap **Save**; tap **Open in Spotify**, then come back to the app.
- [ ] **Podcasts**: play an episode for a minute.
- [ ] **Gallery**: browse an album; if you can upload, try one photo. Check you can't see albums meant for groups you're not in.

## 7. Notifications

- [ ] **Profile → Notification settings**: choose what you'd like by email.
- [ ] Check the bell shows new activity (for example, a reply to your discussion).

## 8. The app on your phone

- [ ] **iPhone (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**. **Android (Chrome):** menu → **Install app**.
- [ ] Open it from your home screen. Does the top show "The Gathering" in full, and does the bottom menu sit on the bottom edge?
- [ ] Scroll a long page and type in a form: does anything get covered by the keyboard or the bottom menu?
- [ ] **More → Sign out**. Reopen the app: you should see the sign-in page and nothing private.

## When you're done

Send any last thoughts through **Report a problem**, and tell us which phone (and iOS/Android version) you used. Thank you!
