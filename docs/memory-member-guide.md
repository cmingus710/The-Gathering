# Memory verses: a guide for members (Phase 9)

Open **Memory Verses** from the home menu (or *Memory verses* in the menu). Your practice and progress are private to you.

## The sections

| Section | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Memory home | The current memory verse, today's reviews and practice, recent verses, group challenges |
| This week | The current assignment with its exact text, translation, and the church's note; coming up and earlier verses |
| Review | Verses due today (in your time zone) and upcoming reviews |
| My Verses | Every verse you're memorizing, from collections or added yourself; removed verses and earlier texts |
| Collections | The church's and your groups' collections, and your own private collections |
| Progress | Verses practiced, review-ready verses, practice over the last 28 days, collections, badges, and how progress works |
| Settings | Difficulty, kids presentation, typing rules, celebrations, daily goal, reminders, pause, download, delete |

The home page shows a small memory card only while a verse is assigned.

## Adding verses

- From a collection or the current assignment: **Add to My Verses** (or **Start learning it**).
- Your own: **Add a verse** → choose the Bible version, book, chapter, and verses → **Show the passage** shows the exact text → **Add to My Verses**. Only versions whose text the church stores can be memorized.

## Bible versions

- **Default memory verse translation** (Practice settings): used for new memory verses; you can choose a different translation for each verse. It's saved to your account, so every device uses it. Until you choose one it follows your Bible reading version, then the church's version. Changing it never changes verses you already have or your earlier results.
- **Licensed versions** (for example NIV, NASB, AMP through API.Bible) are read from an outside service and never stored here. A licensed version appears greyed out under *Not available for memory practice* until the church confirms its terms allow memory practice. Once confirmed, it's marked *licensed, flashcards and self-check only*:
  - The verse is always shown exactly as published, with its copyright.
  - Practice uses flashcards (reference first, then the verse) and self-check.
  - Activities that hide or rearrange words aren't offered.
  - If the service is down, practice says the version is temporarily unavailable; another version is never used instead.
  - If the publisher revises the wording, the verse says so and pauses practice until you choose **Practice the new wording**. Your earlier results stay with the earlier wording.
- **Per verse**: the version selector on *Add a verse* starts at your default; the preview shows exactly the wording you'll practice. If a version doesn't have that passage, you're told so and nothing is added; another version is never used in its place.
- **Assigned verses** say which version they use. *Practiced in WEB, as assigned* means the church requires that version. *You may practice it in another version* means **Start learning it: choose a version** lets you pick yours; the assignment stays the same for everyone else.
- **Change translation** (on a verse's page) shows the current and new wording side by side, any verses the two versions include or number differently, and what happens to your practice before you confirm. The new wording is practiced on its own schedule (practice of one wording isn't practice of another). Your earlier practice stays with the earlier text, badges stay, and switching back brings that progress back. Changing translation never adds practice to a group challenge.
- Every practice activity, check, and history entry uses the verse's own version, shown beside the reference.

## Practicing

Choose an activity, a difficulty (Guided, Growing, Challenge), and, for longer passages, a part. Nothing is timed, nothing makes a sound, and you can go at your own pace.

| Activity | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| Read & repeat | Read it aloud, then hide words step by step and say it again. Show every word whenever you like. |
| Flashcards | See the reference, say the verse, flip the card, and say how well you knew it. Or practice the reference: see the verse, say where it's from. |
| Missing words | Choose (Guided, Growing) or type (Challenge) the hidden words. |
| Word order | Tap pieces in order to rebuild the verse; tap a placed piece to send it back. Works with a keyboard (Tab, then Enter); no dragging. |
| Type the verse | Type it from memory, then see each word compared, with what you typed kept beside it. *Next word* helps if you're stuck. |
| Self-check | See only the reference, recite away from the screen, reveal, and check yourself honestly. |

Hints are part of learning. They're counted only so reviews come back at the right time.

After checking, you'll see **Saved** once the church's server has stored it. If your connection drops, your answer stays on the page and is sent when you're back ("Not saved yet" until then). If you were signed out, sign in in another tab and press *Try again*.

## How progress works

Each practice keeps separate facts: completed, accuracy (for scored activities), hints, your confidence, and the review step. Verses you recall come back after 1, 2, 4, 7, 14, 30, then 60 days. Missing days never lowers anything; verses just wait for you. *Review-ready* means you've recalled a verse on three separate review days; nothing is ever called permanently mastered. Details: [memory-scoring.md](memory-scoring.md).

## Kids presentation

**Settings → Presentation → Kids** gives larger text and buttons, shorter parts, word choices, simple instructions, and gentle celebrations. It only changes how practice looks: it isn't an age setting and changes no one's permissions. If you practice with a child on your account, the progress is yours. To record practice for your child instead, a verified guardian uses **Youth → Family dashboard → Practice verses together** ([guardian-guide.md](guardian-guide.md)).

## Reminders

All off unless you switch them on (Settings): a new weekly verse, verses due for review, a practice reminder on days you haven't practiced, and milestones in challenges you've joined. Pick the time and your time zone. They respect your quiet hours and arrive at most once a day each. Email and push only say a reminder is waiting, never a verse or your results. Change where they go under Notification settings → Memory verses. **Pause** stops them without losing anything.

## Group challenges

Optional. Read *What joining shares*, tick the box, and join. Only a count of practice activities on the challenge's verses (from when you join) is added to a group total, shown once enough people share. Pause sharing or leave at any time; your practice is removed from the total straight away. No leaderboards. See [memory-privacy.md](memory-privacy.md).

## Removing and deleting

- **Remove from My Verses** hides a verse and keeps its history (restore it any time).
- **Delete this verse and its history** deletes your results for that verse permanently. The verse stays in the Bible and in church collections; badges you earned stay.
- **Settings → Delete my practice history** deletes all your verses, results, schedule, and badges (optionally your personal collections). Type the confirmation to proceed.
- **Download my practice data** gives you a JSON file of your verses, results, and badges.

## When a verse's text is updated

If the church or the Bible source updates the wording, the verse says so. **Practice the updated text** starts it fresh; your earlier practice stays with the earlier text under *Removed and earlier texts*. Until you choose, you keep practicing the text you started with. Your Bible version is never changed for you.
