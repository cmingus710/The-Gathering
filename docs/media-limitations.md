# Listening, watching, and outside services: member guide (Phase 7)

## Finding things

- **Teaching & media** (`/media`) links every section and searches devotionals, lessons, videos, podcasts, reading, playlists, and Media gallery album titles you can see, with a filter for each type. Search never includes prayer, care, messages, giving, or journals.
- **Videos** (`/videos`) lists published videos newest first, with a topic filter; each plays right in the list.
- **Devotionals** shows today's devotional (by the church's time zone), with an archive and calendar. If nothing was written for a day, the page says so; the app never invents devotional text.
- **Sunday lessons** shows the latest lesson and a searchable archive (date, speaker, series, topic, book of the Bible).
- **Books** shows this week's assignment, the current book, and past weeks. Mark an assignment done; only you see that.
- **Music & playlists** shows every playlist with its Spotify player right on the page: open Music and press play. **Podcasts** and the **Media gallery** are in the menu and the Teaching & media hub too.

## Playing audio and video

- One thing plays at a time. Starting audio or video pauses whatever else was playing in the app.
- Audio keeps playing in the **mini-player** while you move around the app, above the menu dock. Lock-screen and headphone controls work where the browser supports them (Media Session).
- Captions and transcripts appear when the church provides them.
- Nothing plays until you press play. Videos show a thumbnail (passed through this app, so YouTube or Vimeo isn't contacted yet) and a Play button; pressing Play loads the provider's player in the same place and starts it. On iPhone and iPad, YouTube may need a second tap on its own play button. Spotify players on Music and on a playlist's page load with the page, so opening them connects to Spotify (which may set its own cookies) even before you press play.
- **Saved position:** the app remembers where you stopped. **Saved** (`/saved`) shows your bookmarks and "continue listening." Your history and bookmarks are private: church staff can't see them. **Clear listening history** forgets every saved position.

## Outside services

Restricting a page in this app **doesn't make content on another service private**:

- **YouTube and Vimeo:** a video shown on a members-only page can still be found by anyone with its link on YouTube or Vimeo, unless the church set it to private there (private videos may not play here at all). YouTube is loaded in privacy-enhanced mode, and Vimeo with "do not track"; once you play, the provider's own terms apply.
- **Spotify:** playlists play through Spotify's player. Without a Spotify login in your browser you may hear 30-second previews, and full playback depends on your Spotify account and device. Who can find the playlist is set in Spotify. Spotify's player doesn't tell the app when it starts, so the app treats tapping into a Spotify player as starting it: other players in the app pause, and a Spotify player you'd tapped before reloads (which stops it).
- **Apple Podcasts and other podcasts:** episodes come from the publisher. The church doesn't host or control them.
- **Audible (Books):** the app links to the book on Audible. You need your own Audible account with access to the title (purchase, credit, or membership); the church's copy doesn't give you access. The app never asks for Amazon or Audible sign-in details, and it doesn't embed Audible's player or see your Audible listening.
- **Podcast feed (RSS):** if the church publishes a public podcast feed, only public episodes are in it, and podcast apps that download an episode keep their copy.

## Photos and videos in the Media gallery

Album photos and videos are visible only to members (or one group). Anyone who can see a photo can still screenshot it, so share only photos people are happy to have seen. To ask for a photo to be taken down, open it and choose **Report or ask to remove**.

## Notifications

Choose which new teaching, videos, and photos you hear about under **Notifications → Settings** (Teaching and media, Media gallery, and per-kind switches). Email and lock-screen notices only name the section, never the title or photo. Phone app push arrives with Phase 12; browser push works today where enabled.
