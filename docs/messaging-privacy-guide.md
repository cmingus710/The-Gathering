# Member guide: messages, groups, and your privacy (Phase 5)

Plain-language answers for members. The app links to the same points from **Messages → Settings** and **Community → Guidelines**.

## Who can read what

- **Church-wide discussions:** approved members only. Never public, never shown to visitors or search engines.
- **Group discussions:** only the group's current members. If you leave or are removed, you lose access right away.
- **Direct messages:** only you and the other person. Messages are protected by access controls on our servers; they are **not end-to-end encrypted**. Church administrators can't browse messages. If either of you reports messages, moderators see only the messages selected in that report.
- Your hidden directory details (email, phone, address, birthday) never appear in messages, groups, or search.

## Who can message you

Direct messaging is for adults. It's turned on for your account after a membership administrator confirms it; until then it stays off. You choose at **Messages → Settings**:

- **Approved members** (default): any member with messaging turned on.
- **Only people in my groups:** only someone in a group you're both in right now.
- **No one:** no new messages, even in existing conversations. You can still read your history.

If you've chosen not to be listed in the directory, people can't find you in the message search. Members of a group you share can still message you from the group's member list, if your setting allows it.

## Blocking

Blocking someone stops new direct messages in both directions and hides their message notifications. They aren't told, and they see the same message they'd see if your settings didn't allow messages. Your existing conversation stays. Blocking doesn't hide their posts in discussions or groups you share; use **Report** if a post is a problem.

## Reporting

Use **Report** on a discussion or reply, or **Report messages** in a conversation and select the messages. Moderators see only what you report. The person isn't told who reported them.

## Deleting

- Deleting your discussion or reply erases its text. If others replied, a "deleted" note stays so the conversation still makes sense.
- Edits replace the earlier text.
- **Delete conversation** hides the history from you. When both people have deleted it, the messages are erased. All messages are erased after two years.

## Notifications

Choose what you hear about at **Notifications → Settings**: in the app, by email (right away or as a daily or weekly summary), and optional push notifications on your devices. Emails and push notifications never include message text; they only say there's something new. Quiet hours hold email and push until the morning in your time zone.

Drafts you're writing stay only on the page you're on; they aren't saved in your browser.
