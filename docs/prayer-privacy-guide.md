# Prayer and care: who can see what (member guide)

Plain-language explanation for members. Wording in the app matches this page.

## Before you share

- Share only what you have permission to share, especially about other people.
- Choose the audience carefully.
- Confidential requests are not monitored continuously.
- This is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger, call 911.

## Choosing an audience

| Audience | Who can read it |
| --- | --- |
| **Church members** | Every approved member of the church. Never public, never visitors. |
| **My group** | Current members of the one group you choose. If someone leaves the group, they lose access immediately. |
| **Prayer & care team** | Only the people the church has designated for confidential prayer. Not monitored around the clock. |
| **Private draft** | Only you. Nothing is shared until you choose an audience. |

You always see your own requests (unless your account is suspended). Church staff such as administrators, group leaders, and content editors **don't** get access to group or confidential requests just because of their role. The people who run the church's hosting can technically reach the database; the app's rules govern everyone else.

Before a request is shared, the app shows a preview and asks you to confirm the audience. Requests are never posted to the community discussions automatically.

## Changing the audience later

- **Narrowing** (for example Church → My group, or anything → Private draft) takes effect at once.
- **Widening** (for example My group → Church members) asks you to confirm the new audience.
- When you widen a request, **earlier comments and updates stay with the audience they were written for**. New readers see the request and anything added after the change, plus a note that some earlier items aren't shown. This way nobody's comment reaches more people than they expected.

## Hiding your name

Tick **Hide my name** and readers see "Anonymous". The app's wording:

> Your name is hidden from readers. Designated prayer and care leads can look up who wrote a request when needed, and each lookup is recorded.

Your updates show as "The requester". Your comments on your own request also show as "The requester", unless you tick **Show my name on this comment**; the app warns that readers may then connect you with the request. Making a request anonymous later also hides your name on your earlier comments there. Notifications, emails, and live updates never carry your name or account id.

## Praying and encouraging

- **I'm praying** is one tap, once per person, and you can undo it. Others see only a total ("3 praying"), never a list of names or any ranking.
- Comments are visible to the request's audience. You can edit or delete your own; a deleted comment leaves a "deleted" placeholder.
- The requester can turn comments off; a prayer moderator can also lock them.
- **Follow** a request to hear about new comments and updates. You follow your own requests and ones you comment on automatically.
- **Report** anything inappropriate. A prayer moderator sees only the item you reported.

## Statuses

Open → Answered (with an optional dated update sharing how) → Closed (stays readable, no new comments or prayers) → **Archived** (only you can see it). Restoring from the archive asks you to confirm the audience again. **Delete** removes the request with its updates, comments, and prayers for everyone.

## Notifications

In the app you'll see which request has new activity. Email and push are generic ("New activity on a prayer request") and never include the title, the text, care details, or names. Choose what you receive at **Notifications → Settings → Prayer / Care**. Quiet hours apply.

## Asking for practical help

**Prayer → Request help** sends a private request to the care team for meals, transportation, visits, or other help.

- Only you and the care team see it. It isn't posted as a prayer request or shared with volunteers automatically.
- Please don't include medical records, diagnoses, ID numbers, or other sensitive details.
- Choose how to be contacted: messages in the app, your profile phone number, or your sign-in email. The team sees only the one you choose.
- You'll see the status, your coordinator's name, and their messages. The team's internal notes are kept separately and aren't shown to you.
- **Volunteers:** if the team asks members for help, volunteers see only a general task and time. Only if you allow it (and only volunteers the team accepts) will they see your first name and the details needed, such as a drop-off address. You can turn this off at any time.
- You can **withdraw** a request; it closes and any volunteer requests for it are canceled.

## Helping others

**Prayer → Ways to help** lists care opportunities you can volunteer for. After a coordinator accepts you, you'll see the logistics (if the person agreed to share them). Please keep those details private.

## Drafts on your device

Text you're writing stays only on the page. It isn't saved in your browser's storage, and searches aren't put in the web address.
