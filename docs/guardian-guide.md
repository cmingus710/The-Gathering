# Youth & Families: a guide for parents and guardians

Phase 10. Open **Youth** on the home screen, then **Family dashboard**.

## Getting linked to your child

1. Choose **Link a child**. Enter your child's preferred name (no email or phone number is needed for a child), your relationship, and anything that helps the church office recognize the request. A date of birth is asked only if the church's policy collects it.
2. The church office verifies each parent, legal guardian, or caregiver directly, usually in person. Living in the same household as someone doesn't link you to their children, and nothing is matched automatically.
3. Until then your request shows **Waiting for review**. You won't be told whether the church already has a record for a child. When it's finished it shows **Reviewed**, and if you were linked your child appears on the dashboard.
4. The office chooses what you may do for each child (each one separately): update the profile, register for events, permission forms, photo consent, see learning progress, practice memory verses together, manage their own account, and receive updates. Another guardian may have different permissions. You see only that another verified guardian exists, never their details.

If the office pauses access (for example while something is reviewed), you'll see "access is paused" and nothing else; please contact the office.

## What you can do

- **Profile:** preferred name, participation (taking part or paused), and a date of birth only when the policy collects it. The date of birth is private: never in rosters, searches, logs, or notifications.
- **Groups:** see your child's youth groups; remove them from a group (upcoming registrations for that group's events are canceled).
- **Events:** see upcoming events for your child's groups, read and decide on permission forms, add only the details an event asks for (an emergency contact, pickup notes, anything you choose to share), and register or cancel. If an event is full you join the waitlist and are told if a place opens.
  - Checking a permission statement records your agreement, the time, and the form version. It isn't a legally verified signature.
  - If a form changes in a way that matters, you'll be asked to review it again ("Needs renewed permission").
  - Withdrawing permission keeps the record of your earlier decision and releases your child's place.
  - Event details are seen only by the people staffing that event. Pickup notes are information, not authorization: pickup follows the church's own procedures.
- **Photo consent:** allow or don't allow photos of your child in member and group galleries inside the app. Public use (websites, social media, banners) is separate and offered only if the church's policy allows it. Withdrawing hides published photos staff have linked to your child and sends them for review; it can't recall screenshots or copies people already saved. If guardians disagree, photos aren't published until it's resolved. See [media-consent-workflow.md](media-consent-workflow.md).
- **Practice verses together:** for a child without their own account, practice the memory verses assigned to their group. It's recorded as your child's practice, never yours. Your own memory verses aren't affected.
- **Learning progress:** a summary (verses practiced, ready for review, days practiced). Never answers, journal entries, or reflections.

## Your child's own account (if the church allows it)

Only when the church's approved policy allows independent youth accounts, after the office confirms eligibility:

1. On your child's page, enter **their own** email address and create an invitation. You get a one-time code (shown once). Creating it records your authorization as a verified guardian.
2. Your child signs up with that email, confirms it from their inbox, signs in, and enters the code.
3. You can't sign in as your child and can't see their password, journal, or answers. They choose whether to share a progress summary with you (if the policy offers it).

There are no private messages in the Youth area, between anyone. Group discussions (if the church allows them) are visible to the group's leaders and members, and to guardians only if the policy allows guardians to read them.

## Notifications

Under **Notifications → Settings → Youth & Families**. In the app you'll see your child's preferred name; email and push only say "There's an update in Youth & Families" and never include a child's name, an event, contact details, or form text.
